Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my plan anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

What happens to my listings if I downgrade?

If you downgrade to a plan with fewer listings, your oldest listings exceeding the new limit will be paused. You can choose which ones to keep active.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you are not satisfied with our premium features. Contact support for assistance.

Is there a free trial for agents?

We do not currently offer a free trial for agent plans, but our Basic User plan is free forever.
